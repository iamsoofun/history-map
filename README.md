# NHIRA — National Historical Incident Research & Analysis

**Copyright © 2026 Shane Justin Hladinec. All rights reserved.**

NHIRA is a historical incident research database with an interactive map,
a statistics and research dashboard, a forecast model, and a transparent
research registry that records methodology, frozen specifications, and
findings — including negative and inconclusive results.

## Authorship & provenance

Authorship and the dated integrity chain are documented in
[`PROVENANCE.md`](PROVENANCE.md). Each frozen research finding pins the
SHA-256 hash of the exact dataset it was computed against, so the research
record is self-verifying: any later change to the data is detectable
against the published hashes.

## A note on the forecast

NHIRA's forecast outputs are research artifacts, not predictions of any
specific future event, person, or location. Forecast layers are shown only
after independent, out-of-sample validation; the research registry records
where methods did and did not meet that bar.
